# Friends, emotes and notifications

The social features in game work when you start the game from the SlashClient launcher and are signed in on the launcher's **Friends** tab. Friends are added in the launcher.

## Friends and chat in game

**O** opens the friends window: the list on the left (who is online and where they play), the conversation on the right.

![The friends window with a conversation and a game invite](hud-znajomi.webp)

What you can do there:
- **chat** with friends, groups, your clan and communities – with reactions, replies, and editing or deleting your own messages;
- **invite to play** on the server you are on (**Invite here**), and join a friend's invite with **Join**;
- **invite a party** – several friends at once to the same server;
- **open your world to friends** – friends join your singleplayer world from their friends list, from anywhere;
- **send a screenshot** – encrypted, only you and the friend can see it;
- **accept or decline** friend requests and see **friend suggestions** – SlashClient players you met lately on servers;
- **mute notifications** from one person or conversation.

Community and clan members, requests, bans and settings are managed in the launcher.

## SlashClient notifications

Pop-ups in the corner of the screen: messages, friends coming online, invites, achievements. The **SlashClient notifications** card (Extras) decides which come.

![The notification settings](hud-opcje-notifications.webp)

- Messages from friends and groups, A friend is online, A friend plays on a server, A friend is live, Invites, Communities, Achievements and challenges, Errors, Other (clips, resource packs, LAN world).
- **With the "Do not disturb" status** in the launcher – no notifications from friends and communities.

Move the notifications elsewhere on the screen in the editor (the **Notifications** part).

## Emotes

**N** opens the emote wheel: Hi!, Heart, Haha, GG, Nice, Grr, What?, Fire, Six seven. The emote shows above your head and other SlashClient players see it.

![The emote wheel](hud-emotki.webp)

- **Camera from the front during your own emote** – you see your character from the front for a moment.

## Sprays

The spray key (unbound by default – set it in the Keys window) paints your chosen spray on the block you look at, up to 5 blocks away. Pick the spray in the cosmetics menu (the **Spray** tab). There is a short wait between sprays.

## Cosmetics in game

Pick cosmetics (capes, wings, hats, clothes, pets, effects and more) in SlashClient's main menu, under the skin preview, or in the launcher. The Extras category has switches for what you see on others:

| Card | What it does |
|---|---|
| Other players' cosmetics | all of other players' cosmetics at once (yours always show) |
| Kill effects | effects where opponents fall; yours and other players' apart |
| Shield and weapon skins | other players' skins are off by default – fair in PvP |
| Rings under the feet, Movement effects, Sprays | each apart for you and for others |
| Logo next to names | the SlashClient logo next to players who use it (player list and above heads), and clan tags |

## Above players' heads

- **Show "Live" above players** – a "LIVE" label above friends who are streaming.
- **Players' ping above their heads (on servers)** – every player's ping next to their name.
