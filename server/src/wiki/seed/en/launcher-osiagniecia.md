# Achievements, challenges and Sparks

The **Achievements** tab rewards playing with SlashClient. Achievements and challenges give you **Sparks** (✦) to spend on cosmetics in the [Shop](/wiki/launcher-sklep). Some achievements also unlock special capes.

You need to be signed in to a SlashClient account in the [Friends](/wiki/launcher-znajomi) tab. Play time counts while the launcher is connected to SlashClient.

At the top is the **Your Sparks** card with your balance, and three tabs: **Achievements**, **Challenges** and **Recommend**.

## Achievements

![Achievements](launcher-osiagniecia.webp)

- Tiles at the top: days in a row (with your best), hours played, minutes today, days played and servers.
- Below them, the rules: how many minutes make a day count towards a streak, when a new day starts (midnight UTC) and the most hours that count per day.
- Category filters: all, Play, Streaks, Friends, invites, Groups and clans, Servers, Sharing, Style, Hidden and more, each with a count of what you've earned.
- Each achievement has a description, a progress bar ("14.5 / 50"), a Sparks reward, sometimes a cape, and once earned, the date it was unlocked.
- **Hidden** achievements show a question mark until you earn them.

On unlocking, the launcher shows a notification (a system one when it's in the background). To see a friend's achievements, use the **Achievements** button in their chat.

## Challenges

The **Challenges** tab has two lists: **Today** and **This week**. Each challenge (e.g. "Play 60 min", "Send 5 messages to friends") has a progress bar and a Sparks reward. Finishing every challenge on a list earns an extra **bonus**.

- Daily challenges change at midnight Polish time, weekly ones on Monday; the countdown shows how long is left.
- What counts is what you do after a challenge starts, with the launcher connected to SlashClient; each pays once.

![Daily and weekly challenges](launcher-wyzwania.webp)

## Sparks

Sparks (✦) are SlashClient's currency. You get them for:

- achievements;
- challenges and their bonuses;
- recommending SlashClient to friends;
- rewards from the admins.

Spend them in the [Shop](/wiki/launcher-sklep) on cosmetics. Your balance is in the top-right corner of the launcher, and every change is listed under **Shop → Sparks history**.

## Recommend

The **Recommend** tab (and the **Recommend SlashClient** button in Friends) shows:

- **Your code** and **Your link**, with buttons to copy them;
- **Got a code from a friend?** – a box for a code, which you can enter within a few days of creating your account;
- **Capes for invites** – capes for 1, 3, 5, 10 and 25 referred friends;
- **Invited friends** and where each stands: counted, waiting or rejected.

A referral counts once your friend links a Minecraft account and plays the required time (for example 3 h on at least 2 days). Then you both get Sparks.
