# Chat, groups and pictures

## Messages to friends

Chat with friends in the launcher (**Friends** → click a friend), in the game (the **Friends and chat** key, **O** by default) and in the [phone app](/wiki/strona-aplikacja). A message can be up to 2000 characters. You see when your friend is typing ("typing…") and when they read your message ("Seen").

You can only write to friends.

## Pictures

The picture button (**Send a picture (encrypted)**) or pasting a picture (Ctrl+V) sends a photo or a screenshot. In the game you can send a screenshot taken with F2. The **Media** button gathers all pictures from a chat.

Default limits (the admins can change them):

| Where | One picture | Per day | Kept for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chat with a friend | up to 4 MB | 60 pictures, 100 MB | 30 days |
| Group | – | 30 per person | 30 days |
| Clan | – | 50 per person | 90 days |
| Community | up to 4 MB | 50 per person | 365 days |

## GIFs and stickers

The **GIF** button opens a search for GIFs and stickers (from GIPHY). The SlashClient server fetches them for you, so GIPHY never sees your IP address. In the game a GIF shows as "[GIF] title", and a click shows a frame of it. The button disappears when the server has GIFs switched off.

## Editing and deleting

Hover over your message and open the **…** menu:

- **Edit** – fix the text within 15 minutes of sending (by default). The message gets "(edited)". Pictures and invites cannot be edited.
- **Delete** – the message disappears for both of you, and "Message deleted" stays in its place.

In the game: **Edit** / **Delete**; Enter saves, Esc cancels.

## Groups

A group chat for several friends. In **Friends**, in the **Groups** section, click **+** (**New group**), name it and pick friends.

- Up to 16 people including you, up to 30 groups per player (by default).
- A new member sees messages from the moment they joined.
- Only the group's founder removes members.

## Encryption

Chats with friends, groups and the clan chat are end-to-end encrypted (the lock icon). The SlashClient server stores them but cannot read them. Community chat is **not** encrypted – the launcher says so.

The settings are in **Friends** → **Account and profile** → **Chat encryption**:

- **This device's code** and the **Devices** list – remove a device you no longer use.
- When you sign in on a new computer, your other launchers ask whether to share your chat history with it. Compare the device code on both screens.
- A **Chat key backup** protected with a **Chat password** (at least 10 characters). With it you can read your whole history on a new computer. The password cannot be recovered – write it down.

## Anti-spam limits

Writing too fast in one chat shows "Slow down a little — you can write again in … s". By default that is 6 messages per 10 seconds in a chat and 40 per minute in total.
