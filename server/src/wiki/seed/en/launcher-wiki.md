# The Wiki tab

The **Wiki** tab lets you look up a recipe or a mob without leaving the launcher. The switch in the top-right corner picks the source, and the launcher remembers your last choice.

![The Wiki tab with a minecraft.wiki article](launcher-wiki.webp)

## minecraft.wiki

- Type the name of a block, item or mob into the search box – suggestions appear as you type.
- The **For example:** chips (Crafting Table, Creeper, Diamond…) open popular articles.
- An article shows the description, the info table and the recipes; the sections about obtaining and crafting open by themselves. Links inside an article open further articles, and the arrow goes back.
- The **minecraft.wiki** button opens the article in your browser.

> The content comes from minecraft.wiki in English (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 licence), so search with English names.

## SkyUp.pl

The SkyUp.pl server wiki: articles by category and a search box. Results come in two groups: **Articles** (matching titles) and **In the content** (full-text search). **Open in browser** opens the article on the server's website.
