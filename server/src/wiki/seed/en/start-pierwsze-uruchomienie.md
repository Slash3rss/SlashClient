# First launch

On the first start the launcher opens a wizard that gets everything ready to play in about a minute. You can skip any step and change it later in **Settings**, **Accounts** or **Profiles**.

## The wizard's steps

### 1. Welcome

Pick the launcher's language (Polish or English) and click **Let's go**.

### 2. Minecraft account

Sign in with the Microsoft account that owns Minecraft, or add an **offline account** with just a name.

- **Microsoft**: the launcher opens Microsoft's page and copies the code to your clipboard right away. Paste it (Ctrl+V), sign in and come back – the launcher finishes by itself. It never sees your password.
- **Offline**: just a name. Works in single player and on offline servers, not on premium servers.

You can add and switch accounts later in the **Accounts** tab.

### 3. Import from another launcher

If the computer has profiles from other launchers (Prism, MultiMC, CurseForge, Modrinth App, the official launcher, Dawn, Feather), the wizard lists them. Tick the ones to bring over: mods, worlds and settings are copied, and the originals stay untouched. This step only appears when something is found.

### 4. First profile

A profile is a game version with its own mods, worlds and settings. The wizard suggests what we recommend – just click **Next**.

| Setting | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Minecraft version** | The latest release or a version you pick. Versions with the HUD are marked *SlashClient HUD*. |
| **Kind of game** | **Fabric + SlashClient HUD** (recommended: mods, the HUD, cosmetics and friends in game) or **Plain game (vanilla)**. |
| **Performance mods** | Sodium, Lithium and the like, installed right away (Fabric only). |
| **Memory (RAM)** | How much memory the game gets. Below the slider you see what we recommend for your computer. |
| **Mode for slower computers** | Ready-made settings for weaker hardware (e.g. a Raspberry Pi). The wizard suggests one. |

### 5. Comfort

- **Fewer animations** – a lighter launcher on a slower computer.
- **Hide the launcher while playing** – it comes back when the game closes.
- **Show on Discord what you are playing** – a status on your Discord profile.
- **Launcher window at start**.
- On Linux: **Desktop shortcut**.

### 6. Friends and cosmetics

Sign in to your SlashClient account with one click, using the Minecraft account from step 2. A SlashClient account means friends, chat and game invites, plus cosmetics other players can see. More: [SlashClient account](/wiki/start-konto).

### 7. All set!

The wizard shows a summary. Click **Play** – the first start downloads the game, so it takes a moment.

> The game will not start? See the [FAQ](/wiki/faq-czeste-pytania).
