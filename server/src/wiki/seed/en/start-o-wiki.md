# How to use the wiki

## Search

Type a few letters into **Search the wiki…**. Results show up right away: pages with a matching title first, then those with a matching description and headings.

## Polish version

Every page has a **Polski** link under its title. A page we have not translated yet shows its Polish version.

## The wiki in the launcher

| Where | What you see |
|---|---|
| Launcher → **Wiki** → **SlashClient** | the same pages, with pictures and search |
| [slashclient.pl/wiki](/wiki) | the wiki on the website |

### Links

Links to other wiki pages open in the launcher, links to other websites open in your browser.
