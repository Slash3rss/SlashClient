# Accounts, skins and cosmetics

The **Accounts** tab holds your Minecraft accounts, your skin in 3D and your SlashClient cosmetics.

![The Accounts tab with cosmetics](launcher-konta.webp)

## Adding accounts

![Accounts and adding a new one](launcher-konta-logowanie.webp)

### Microsoft account

The official Minecraft account – needed for premium servers.

1. Click **Sign in with Microsoft**.
2. The launcher opens Microsoft's page and copies the code to your clipboard by itself.
3. Paste the code (Ctrl+V), sign in with the account that owns Minecraft and come back – the launcher finishes on its own.

### Offline account

Just a name (up to 16 characters). Works in singleplayer and on offline servers. Type the name under **Offline account** and click add.

In the account list, the use button picks the account to play with and **Delete** removes it. You can also tie an account to a profile for good (see [Profiles](/wiki/launcher-profile)).

## Skin viewer

At the top is your character in 3D with your current skin, cape and SlashClient cosmetics.

- **Zoom in** / **Zoom out** (plus and minus under the figure), and drag to turn it;
- **Walk** – a walking animation;
- **Elytra** / **Cape** – shows the cape as an elytra;
- **Download PNG** – saves the skin file;
- **Skin editor** – opens the editor.

Microsoft accounts also get **Change skin…** (from a file, with the **Steve (classic)** or **Alex (slim)** model) and **Default skin**. An offline account shows the skin of the premium player with the same name, or the default one – on offline servers other players see the default skin.

## Skin editor

The skin editor paints on a 64×64 skin with a live 3D preview.

![The skin editor](launcher-edytor-skina.webp)

- Tools: **Pencil**, **Eraser**, **Fill**, **Colour picker**, **Undo** (Ctrl+Z) and **Redo** (Ctrl+Y).
- Layers: **Both layers**, **Inner**, **Outer**; the Steve or Alex model; **Grid**; recent colours.
- The right mouse button erases, Alt + click picks a colour.
- When you're done: **Download PNG**, **Save to library** or **Apply to account** (Microsoft account).

## Skin library

The **Skin library** keeps your favourite skins so you can switch between them in one click. **Save current** adds your current look, **Add from file…** adds PNG files. Click a skin to rename it or change its model, and **Apply to account** puts it on your Microsoft account.

![The skin library](launcher-biblioteka-skinow.webp)

## Cosmetics

The **Cosmetics** part has two tabs: **SlashClient** (cosmetics SlashClient players see in the game) and **Minecraft** (your Microsoft account's capes – pick one and click apply).

### SlashClient cosmetics

They need a SlashClient sign-in in the [Friends](/wiki/launcher-znajomi) tab.

- Slot tabs: **Cape**, **Wings**, **Elytra**, **Trail**, **Aura**, **Pet**, **Hat**, **Outfit**, **Kill effect**, **Suit**, **Shield**, **Weapon skin**, **Shoes**, **Hands**, **Face**, **Ears & horns**, **Back**, **Tail**, **Ring**, **Movement effect**, **Spray**, **Prop** and **Sets**. A green dot means you're wearing something in that slot.
- The first tile, **Nothing**, takes the item off. A lock marks an item still to get: a Sparks price (clicking buys it from the shop), an achievement, a rank, project supporters, or a grant from the admins. The text under the grid says how to get it.
- The figure puts the item on right away; kill and movement effects play on the preview.
- The **Shop** link with your Sparks balance opens the [Shop](/wiki/launcher-sklep).

### The whole account's set and a character's set

Above the tabs it says whose set you're editing: **The whole account's set** or a character's set (with several Minecraft accounts, each can wear something different). **Same as the account** gives the character the whole account's set again.

### Sets

The **Sets** tab shows items that go together, a count of the parts you own and a **Wear the set** button. Hovering a set tries it on the figure.
