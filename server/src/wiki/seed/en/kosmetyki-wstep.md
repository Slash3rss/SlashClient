# Cosmetics: how to wear them

SlashClient cosmetics are capes, wings, pets, outfits, effects and other extras for your character. Other SlashClient players see them on every server – even on servers without any mods. Players without SlashClient see your normal look.

Cosmetics give no advantage in the game: they do not change hitboxes, stats or items.

> To wear cosmetics you need a [SlashClient account](/wiki/start-konto) and the game started from the SlashClient launcher on a version with [SlashClient HUD](/wiki/start-wersje).

## Kinds (slots)

You wear one item per slot. These pages cover every slot:

| Page | Slots |
|---|---|
| [Capes, wings and elytras](/wiki/kosmetyki-peleryny) | Cape, Wings, Elytra |
| [Outfits and things on your character](/wiki/kosmetyki-ubrania) | Hat, Outfit, Suit, Shoes, Hands, Face, Ears & horns, Back, Tail |
| [Effects](/wiki/kosmetyki-efekty) | Trail, Aura, Ring, Movement effect, Kill effect |
| [Pets, items and sprays](/wiki/kosmetyki-pupile-i-przedmioty) | Pet, Shield, Weapon skin, Spray, Prop |

On top of that there are [Sets](/wiki/kosmetyki-zestawy): items that go together, put on in one click.

## In the launcher

1. Open the **Accounts** tab.
2. Below the character preview, in the **Cosmetics** box, pick the **SlashClient** tab (the **Minecraft** tab holds your Microsoft account's official capes).
3. Click a slot (e.g. **Cape**) and then an item. The **Nothing** tile takes off what you wear in that slot.

The 3D preview shows your character with the cosmetics right away. **Walk** shows how they move, and for a tail or a back item the figure turns by itself. Slots where you wear something have a green dot.

Items you do not have yet show a lock. Hover over one to see how to get it; shop items show their price in Sparks, and a click asks whether to buy. More: [How to get cosmetics](/wiki/kosmetyki-zdobywanie).

## In the game

Change cosmetics without leaving the game:

- in the pause menu (Esc) click **Cosmetics**,
- or in the SlashClient main menu click **Cosmetics** under your character.

The window has the same slots and sets as the launcher, your Sparks in the corner and a preview of your character in the world. Drag the character with the mouse to turn it.

## On the website

On [slashclient.pl/account](https://slashclient.pl/account) open the **Cosmetics** tab. Hovering over an item tries it on the 3D figure, and a click puts it on. The change shows the next time you join the game.

![Cosmetics on the account page](strona-konto-kosmetyki.webp)

## The whole account's set and a character's set

By default all your Minecraft accounts wear **the whole account's set**. Each Minecraft account linked to your SlashClient account can have its own set, though:

- **in the launcher** – pick the Minecraft account in the list in **Accounts**; above the slots it says "Set of …";
- **on the website** – pick the account in the **Set for:** list;
- **in the game** – you change the set of the character you are playing.

The **Same as the account** button goes back to the whole account's set.

## Who sees them

Every SlashClient player who has not switched them off sees capes, wings, outfits and pets. Shields and weapon skins are seen by others only when they turn them on, and sprays, rings and effects have switches of their own. Details: [Who sees cosmetics](/wiki/kosmetyki-widocznosc).
