# A player's public profile

Every SlashClient player has a profile on the website at **slashclient.pl/u/*name***. It shows the character in its cosmetics, stats, achievements and more – as much as the player wants to show.

![A player's public profile (Polish interface)](strona-profil.webp)

## What is on the profile

- a 3D character wearing its cosmetics, the name, ranks, the supporter badge and the supporters' avatar frame;
- the [clan](/wiki/spolecznosc-klany) and [communities](/wiki/spolecznosc-spolecznosci) (links to their pages), Minecraft account names;
- "On SlashClient since …" and "Online now" or "Last online: …";
- stats: hours played, the current and the longest streak, days played, servers, Sparks, friends, achievements;
- the **Achievements**, **Cosmetics**, **Servers** and **Streams** sections (with the player when you are live).

On your own profile you also get **As others see it** (a preview of what others see) and **Privacy**. Anyone can copy the link with **Copy link**.

## How to open your profile

- **Website**: [Account](https://slashclient.pl/account) → **Privacy** → **See your profile**.
- **Launcher**: **Friends** → **Account and profile** → **Profile on the website** (**See your profile**, **Copy link**).
- Open a friend's profile with **Show profile** in your chat with them.

## Profile privacy

On your [account](https://slashclient.pl/account), in the **Privacy** tab (or in the launcher's **Profile privacy**):

### Who sees the profile

| Option | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| **Everyone** (the default) | anyone with the link, even without signing in |
| **Friends** | only your SlashClient friends (signed in) |
| **Only me** | nobody but you |

### What is on the profile

| Item | By default |
|---|---|
| Stats (hours played, streaks, days played) | on |
| Achievements | on |
| Worn cosmetics | on |
| Clan | on |
| Communities (only public ones you leave on in the launcher) | on |
| Minecraft accounts (names) | on |
| Number of friends | on |
| Servers you play on | **off** |
| When you were last online | **off** |
| Streams | on |

**Let search engines (Google etc.) show my profile** is off by default and only works with **Everyone**.

> You always see your whole profile. To check what others see, click **As others see it**.

## When a profile is hidden

A visitor sees "Private profile", "Friends only" (with a sign-in button) or "Player not found". Moderators can hide a profile that breaks the rules – its owner then sees a message about it.
