# The slashclient.pl website

On [slashclient.pl](https://slashclient.pl/en) you download the launcher, sign in to your account, see player profiles and shared things, report bugs and read what is new. The site is in Polish and English (the English version: [slashclient.pl/en](https://slashclient.pl/en)); switch the language in the footer.

![The home page (Polish version)](strona-glowna.webp)

## The home page

- The top bar: **Features**, **HUD**, **Cosmetics**, **FAQ**, **Bug reports**, **Support us**, and on the right **Sign in** (or your name) and **Download**.
- A **New** pill with the latest notable change – it leads to the [changelog](/wiki/strona-zmiany).
- A **Download for …** button matching your system and the **Other systems:** line – see [Download and install](/wiki/start-instalacja).
- A tour of the features: the HUD, friends, the [phone app](/wiki/strona-aplikacja), cosmetics (the **Choose a cape** button leads to your account), the benchmark and clips.
- An **FAQ** with the most common questions and a **Download SlashClient** section at the bottom.
