# Who sees cosmetics

## SlashClient players

Other players who play through SlashClient with the HUD mod see your cosmetics – on every server, even ones without mods. Players without SlashClient see your normal skin and your normal Minecraft cape.

A change made on the website shows the next time you join the game.

## What others see by default

| Kind | Do others see it by default? |
|---|---|
| Cape, wings, elytra, pet, outfit, suit, shoes, hands, face, ears, back, tail, prop | yes |
| Trail and aura | yes |
| Ring, movement effect, kill effect | yes (anyone can switch them off) |
| Spray | yes, on the same server, for a minute |
| **Shield and weapon skin** | **no** – only players who turn them on themselves (fair PvP) |

## Switching off other players' cosmetics

Everything is in the HUD menu (Right Shift) → the **Extras** category:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Other players' cosmetics** | Switches off all other players' cosmetics at once. Yours stay. |
| **Shield and weapon skins** → **Other players' skins (fair PvP: off)** | Shows other players' shields and weapons. Off by default. |
| **Kill effects** → **Other players' effects** | Other players' kill effects. The main switch turns off yours too. |
| **Rings under the feet** → **Other players' rings** | Other players' rings. The main switch turns off yours too. |
| **Movement effects** → **Other players' effects** | Other players' movement effects. |
| **Sprays** → **Other players' sprays** | Other players' sprays. |

> These switches only work for you. Other players still see your cosmetics the way they set it up themselves.

## Clan tags and the logo next to nicks

SlashClient players have the SlashClient logo (in their rank's colour) and their clan tag next to their nick. Switch them off in the same place: **Logo next to names**, with **Clan tags next to names** and **Other players' clan tags** inside. See [Clans](/wiki/spolecznosc-klany).
