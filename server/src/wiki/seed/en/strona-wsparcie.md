# Supporting the project

SlashClient is free and will stay free. The server with friends, chat, screenshots and profiles runs around the clock and costs money every month – if you want to help keep it going, you can support the project voluntarily at [slashclient.pl/support](https://slashclient.pl/support).

![The support page (Polish version)](strona-wsparcie.webp)

## Fair rules

- Support gives no advantage in the game.
- There are no loot boxes.
- [Sparks](/wiki/spolecznosc-iskry) cannot be bought – you only earn them by playing.
- Nothing is taken away from players who do not support.

## Tiers

The default tiers (the team can change them; the page shows the current prices):

| Tier | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Supporter** | the "Supporter" rank next to your name, the **SlashClient Heart** cape, an avatar frame and a "Supporting since …" badge on your profile, 2× more space for cloud profiles and screenshots |
| **Supporter+** | all of the above, the **Patron's wings** elytra, early access to new versions (test builds), 3× more space |
| **Patron** | all of the above, the golden **Patron** cape, a golden avatar frame, 4× more space |

You can support **one-time** (for 1, 2, 3, 6 or 12 periods, with no renewal) or **monthly**. Prices are in złoty or euro.

## How to support

1. Sign in on the website.
2. On the support page pick **One-time** or **Monthly** and a tier.
3. Tick the required consents and click the support button.
4. Pay by card, BLIK, Przelewy24, Apple Pay or Google Pay (Stripe), or with PayPal (one-time only).

The supporters' rank and items are active while your support lasts.

## After supporting

On your [account](https://slashclient.pl/account), in the **Support** tab:

- your tier and when it ends, **Support again**;
- **Manage subscription** – change or cancel monthly support;
- **Show the supporter badge on my profile** and **Show me on the supporters wall** (the wall on the support page shows only those who agree);
- **Payment history** with receipts and a refund request button.

In the launcher: **Settings** → **Support the project**.
