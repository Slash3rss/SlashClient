# Friends and statuses

Your SlashClient friends are the same in the launcher, the game and the [phone app](/wiki/strona-aplikacja). You see who is playing and on which server, chat with them and invite them to play in one click.

> You need a [SlashClient account](/wiki/start-konto). In the launcher, sign in on the **Friends** tab.

## Where it is

- **Launcher**: the **Friends** tab.
- **Game**: the **Friends and chat** key (**O** by default) – friend list, chat, invites, groups, clan and communities. The game has to be started from a launcher you are signed in to.
- **Phone**: the [app](/wiki/strona-aplikacja), **Friends** tab.

![The friend list in the phone app (Polish interface)](spolecznosc-znajomi-telefon.webp)

## Adding friends

Type your friend's SlashClient name into **Friend's name** and click the add button. Your friend gets an invite to accept or decline. If they already invited you, you become friends right away.

The list has sections:

- **Invites** – invites to you, with accept and decline buttons;
- **Friends · N online** – those in a game first, then online, then offline;
- **Sent invites** – you can cancel them.

## Statuses

Click the dot on your avatar to change your status:

| Status | What friends see |
|---|---|
| **Online** | a green dot and what you are doing |
| **Away** | a yellow dot |
| **Do not disturb** | a red dot |
| **Invisible** | they see you as offline |

Under your name friends see what you are doing, e.g. "Online in the launcher", "Minecraft 1.21.11 · in the menu", "· single player", "· on *server address*", "Online on the phone" or "Offline · 2 h ago". On partner servers the status can also show the game mode or sector.

![Your status in the phone app (Polish interface)](spolecznosc-status-telefon.webp)

### Privacy

In **Friends** → the gear icon **Account and profile** → **Privacy and notifications**:

- **Show friends which server I play on** – when off, friends do not see the server address (also on the [account page](/wiki/strona-konto));
- **Notify me when friends start playing**.

## Invite to play

In a chat with a friend click **Invite to play**. They get an **Invite to play** card with a **Join** button – the launcher starts the game and joins the same server. You invite to the server you are on, or type an address. A single-player world opened to LAN can be joined through SlashClient, even from another network.

An invite expires after a while (30 minutes by default).

## Party

**Invite party** invites several friends at once – up to 7 people besides you. Everyone sees who is in the party, who is invited and who declined. The one who made it can disband it; the others can leave.

## Friend suggestions

Under **Suggestions** the launcher shows SlashClient players you played with on the same server or stood next to in the world ("You played together on …"), and mutual friends. Click **Add** or **Don't suggest again**.

Do not want to be suggested? Turn off **Show me in friend suggestions** (on the [account page](/wiki/strona-konto), **Privacy** tab). Then you see no suggestions either, and stored meetings are deleted.

## Popular among friends

In the mods, packs and servers views the launcher shows what your friends use. You decide whether your mods and servers count there: **Show friends what I use** – **Nobody**, **All friends** or **Picked friends**.

## Friends on the minimap

You can show your position on your friends' minimap: **Show my position on friends' minimap** – **Nobody (off)** (the default), **All friends** or **Picked friends**. It only works on the same server and in the same dimension, and positions are never stored.

## Other buttons in a chat

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Show profile** | Your friend's [public profile](/wiki/strona-profil) |
| **Media** | Pictures sent in the chat |
| **Achievements** | Your friend's achievements |
| **Mute notifications** | Messages still arrive, without notifications |
| **Watch** | When your friend is [streaming](/wiki/spolecznosc-streamy) |
| **Remove friend** | Removes the friend |
