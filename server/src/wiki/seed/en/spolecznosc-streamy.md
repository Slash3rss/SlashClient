# Streams

Link your **Twitch** or **Kick** channel to your SlashClient account. When you start streaming Minecraft, your friends see it on their friend list, in the game and on your profile.

## Linking a channel

- **Launcher**: **Friends** → the gear icon **Account and profile** → **Streams** → **Link Twitch** or **Link Kick**.
- **Website**: [Account](https://slashclient.pl/account) → the **Streams** tab.

This is not a way of signing in – the linked channel is only used to show that you are live. Unlink it in the same place.

![The Streams tab on the account page (Polish interface)](strona-konto-streamy.webp)

## What friends see

- A red **LIVE on Twitch · stream title** badge on their friend list and in the chat header, with a **Watch** button.
- One notification when you start a stream.
- In the game: a "… is live" notification, a LIVE line on the friend list and a LIVE tag above your head (if they have it turned on in the HUD extras).
- On your [public profile](/wiki/strona-profil): the stream player or a link to your channel.

Only Minecraft streams count.

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Hide from friends that I am streaming** | Friends see no LIVE badge and get no notifications. |
| **Notify me when friends start streaming** | A notification when a friend starts a stream. |

Switch off streams on your profile in your [profile privacy](/wiki/strona-profil) settings.
