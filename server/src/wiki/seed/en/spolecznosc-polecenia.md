# Recommend SlashClient

Every player has their own code and link for recommending SlashClient. When a friend starts playing with your code, you both get [Sparks](/wiki/spolecznosc-iskry), and further referrals earn you capes.

## Your code and link

In the launcher open **Friends** and click **Recommend SlashClient** in the top right corner. The same is in **Achievements**, on the **Recommend** tab. You see:

- your **code** – 6 characters with no easily confused letters and digits (no I, L, O, 0 or 1);
- a **link** `slashclient.pl/z/CODE` – to send to a friend;
- the people you referred and what each of them still needs.

The link opens a page with your invite, a download button and the code to copy.

![The invite page from a player (Polish interface)](spolecznosc-polecenie-strona.webp)

## What your friend does

1. Downloads SlashClient and signs in with a Minecraft account.
2. Enters your code – the launcher asks for it after the first sign-in ("Got a code from a friend?"), and anyone who came through your link has it filled in already. The code can be entered within 7 days of creating the account, once.
3. Plays – the referral counts once they link a Minecraft account (one that has never counted for anyone) and play 3 hours on at least 2 different days.

Each referral on the list has a state: **Waiting**, **Counted** or **Rejected** (the team rejects referrals that try to cheat).

## Rewards

| Who | Reward (by default) |
|---|---|
| You, for each counted referral | 100 Sparks |
| Your referred friend | 50 Sparks to start with |

Up to 20 referrals count per month; the rest wait for the next month. On top of that there are [achievements](/wiki/spolecznosc-osiagniecia) with capes:

| Referrals | Achievement | Cape |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Word of mouth | Sakura |
| 3 | Recruiter | Zorza (Aurora) |
| 5 | Referral crew | Tęcza (Rainbow) |
| 10 | Influencer | Wir (Vortex) |
| 25 | SlashClient ambassador | Gwiezdny deszcz (Starfall) |

Your referred friend gets the **Referred** achievement.

> A **community** invite (a `/c/…` link) is something else – see [Communities](/wiki/spolecznosc-spolecznosci).
