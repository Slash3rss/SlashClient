# Statistics and session summary

The launcher records every game session: when you played, for how long, with which profile and on which server. It all stays on your computer.

## Statistics

**Where:** Profiles → the **Statistics** button above the profile list.

![Game statistics](launcher-statystyki.webp)

- Range: **7 days**, **30 days**, **90 days** or **All**.
- Cards at the top: total time (across all profiles), time in the period, sessions (average and longest) and your streak (days in a row with play).
- **Play time by day** – a bar chart.
- **Profiles** and **Servers** – where you play the most (time and number of sessions or joins).
- **Times of day** – the hours you usually play.
- **Recent sessions** – the last ten summaries; **All sessions** opens the full list with profile and place filters, totals and a way to remove entries.

## Session summary

After you close the game, the launcher shows a **Session summary** window. Clicking a session under **Recent sessions** opens the same window.

![Session summary](launcher-podsumowanie-sesji.webp)

The summary shows:

- the session's length, profile, version and times;
- **Where you played** – servers and worlds with the time spent in each;
- **Highlights** – kills, deaths, K/D, damage dealt, blocks (mined and placed), distance, messages and average FPS;
- **Screenshots and clips** from this session;
- **Sparks and achievements** and **Challenges** earned meanwhile (when you're signed in to SlashClient);
- your streak, and whether this was your longest session in the last 30 days.

> The in-game numbers (kills, blocks, distance, FPS) come from SlashClient HUD, so they're only there for profiles with the SlashClient mod on.

**How to turn it off:** Settings → untick **Show a session summary after the game closes**, or click **Do not show again** in the window itself.
