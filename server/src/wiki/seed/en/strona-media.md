# Screenshots and clips

Share a screenshot or a clip from the game with one link, **slashclient.pl/media/…**. Whoever gets the link watches it in a browser, without SlashClient and without signing in. On Discord and similar apps the link shows a preview.

![A shared screenshot (Polish interface)](strona-media.webp)

## How to share

1. In the launcher open a profile and its **Screenshots** or **Clips** tab.
2. Next to a screenshot or a clip click the **Share by link** icon.
3. You can add a title (up to 80 characters). You can trim a clip first (**Trim**).
4. The link is copied to your clipboard.

You need a [SlashClient account](/wiki/start-konto). See and delete all your links in **My links**.

## What the page shows

- **Screenshot** or **Clip**, the title, the author (`by @name`), the size, when it was sent and when it disappears;
- a clip plays in the browser;
- the **Copy link** and **Download** buttons and – for the author – **Delete**.

## Limits

The defaults (the admins can change them):

| What | How much |
|---|---|
| How long a link works | 30 days |
| Largest screenshot | 10 MB (PNG or JPEG) |
| Largest clip | 90 MB |
| Active links per player | 50 |
| Space per player | 500 MB |

[Supporters of the project](/wiki/strona-wsparcie) get 2, 3 or 4 times as many links and as much space, depending on their tier.

Your first share earns the **Photographer** achievement.
