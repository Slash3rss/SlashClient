# Outfits and things on your character

These cosmetics are made of small blocks laid over your skin, so they look like part of the character. Most of them hide under real armour, so they never cover what you actually wear in the game.

![Outfits, hats, face, ears, hands and shoes](kosmetyki-ubrania-ikony.webp)

## Hat

Something on your head: a top hat, a crown, a cap, headphones, a beanie, a wizard's hat, a halo and more. It hides under a real helmet.

![The Hat slot](kosmetyki-hat.webp)

## Outfit

Clothes over your skin: a scarf, a bow tie, a tie, a medal, a jacket. It hides under a real chestplate.

![The Outfit slot](kosmetyki-top.webp)

## Suit

A costume for the whole body: astronaut, samurai armour, panda, dinosaur, knight, a ghost in a sheet, a mech, a pirate. It hides your hat and outfit, and real armour hides the part of the suit it covers.

![The Suit slot](kosmetyki-suit.webp)

![All suits](kosmetyki-stroje-ikony.webp)

## Shoes

Shoes on both feet: sneakers, hiking boots, slippers, bunny slippers, rocket boots, roller skates, flippers. They hide under real boots.

![The Shoes slot](kosmetyki-shoes.webp)

## Hands

Something on your hands or wrist: boxing gloves, a watch, a bracelet, mittens, a ring and more. You see them in first person too.

![The Hands slot](kosmetyki-hands.webp)

## Face

Glasses, a mask or a moustache. Glasses stay on under a helmet; masks and goggles hide.

![The Face slot](kosmetyki-face.webp)

## Ears & horns

Ears or horns on your head: cat, fox, bunny and elf ears, devil horns, antlers, a unicorn horn, earmuffs and more. Ears twitch every now and then. They hide under a helmet and wherever they would poke through a hat.

![The Ears & horns slot](kosmetyki-ears.webp)

## Back

Something on your back: a backpack, a quiver, a katana, a guitar, a jetpack, a small chest, a shield and more. It takes the place of the cape and the wings (they are hidden) and hides under a real elytra.

![The Back slot](kosmetyki-back.webp)

## Tail

A tail swings as you walk. Under a cape it hangs lower. There are cat, fox, kitsune, devil, dragon, wolf and squirrel tails and more.

![The Tail slot](kosmetyki-tail.webp)

![Back items and tails](kosmetyki-plecy-ogony-ikony.webp)

## How to put them on

Launcher: **Accounts** → **Cosmetics** → **SlashClient** and the slot; in the game **Esc → Cosmetics**; on the website [Account](https://slashclient.pl/account) → **Cosmetics**. Most of them are free, some are sold in the [shop](/wiki/kosmetyki-zdobywanie) – the price is on the tile. Item names are shown in Polish.
