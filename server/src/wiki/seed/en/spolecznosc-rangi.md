# Ranks and the badge next to your name

SlashClient players have a small SlashClient logo next to their name – in the game above heads and on the player list, on friend lists and in chats. The logo's colour tells the player's rank.

## Ranks

| Rank | Colour | Who has it |
|---|---|---|
| **Admin** | red | the team running SlashClient |
| **Support** | blue | people who help players |
| **Partner** | yellow | partners, e.g. owners of partner servers |
| **Friend** | green or pink | friends of the project |
| **Supporter** | the support tier's colour | [supporters of the project](/wiki/strona-wsparcie), while their support lasts |

The team can add more ranks. Some ranks have their own [cosmetics](/wiki/kosmetyki-zdobywanie), e.g. a cape in their colour. In the launcher and on the website a rank can have its own icon instead of the logo; in the game it is always the logo in the rank's colour.

## Which rank you show

Have several ranks? Pick what shows next to your name. On the [account page](/wiki/strona-konto), in the **Profile** tab, set **Badge next to the nick:**

- **Most important rank** (the default),
- one of your ranks,
- **No rank badge**.

With several Minecraft accounts, each can show a different badge (**In game as …**). Your [public profile](/wiki/strona-profil) shows all your ranks.

## Switching off the logo next to names

In the game: HUD menu (Right Shift) → **Extras** → **Logo next to names**. It also turns off clan tags. See [Who sees cosmetics](/wiki/kosmetyki-widocznosc).
