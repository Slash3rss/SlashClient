# Groups, clans and communities

Besides one-to-one chats, the **Friends** tab has three kinds of shared chat. Each has its own section in the list.

| | Group | Clan | Community |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who | chosen friends | one clan per player | up to about a thousand people |
| Encryption | yes, like a friend chat | – | no |
| Tag next to your name | – | yes, e.g. [SLSH] | – |
| Channels and roles | – | owner, officers, members | channels, roles, moderation |

## Groups

A group is a chat with several friends at once, encrypted just like a friend chat.

**How to create one:** Friends → the **Groups** section → **+** (**New group**) → tick friends → create the group.

The group header has **Media**, **Add friends**, rename and **Leave group** buttons.

![A group chat](launcher-grupa.webp)

## Clans

A clan has a short tag (2–5 characters) shown next to your name in the launcher and in the game, and a chat of its own.

**How to found one:** Friends → the **Clan** section → **Found a clan** → enter a tag (e.g. WOLF) and a name → confirm. You join a clan by invitation ("… invites you to a clan" → join / decline).

In **Manage clan** (the gear next to the section):

- the member list: make or unmake officers, hand the clan over, kick members;
- **Invite to clan** (owner and officers);
- **Tag and name** and **Clan logo** (a PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF image);
- hide the clan tag next to your own name;
- leave the clan, or for the owner, delete it.

## Communities

A community is a large chat with channels – for a server, a building crew or fans of a mod.

![A community with channels](launcher-spolecznosc.webp)

Buttons next to the **Communities** section:

- **Join with an invite** – paste an invite link;
- **Discover communities** – public communities to browse;
- **Create a community** – name, description, visibility (public – listed in Discover, or private – invite only) and how people join (open, or by request – moderators accept).

### Channels and messages

- Channels are tabs under the name: **General**, **Announcements** (only admins post there) and channels the owner adds.
- Messages can be replied to, edited, copied, pinned, reported and reacted to.
- In the header: **Members**, **Pinned messages** and **Notifications** (all messages, mentions only (@name) or none).

### The "More" menu

- **Media** and **New poll** (2–10 answers, optionally several choices and an end time);
- for moderators: **Join requests**, **Reports**, **Invite links** (with expiry and a use limit) and **Slow mode** (from 5 seconds to an hour between messages);
- **Community settings**: **Channels and roles** (custom roles with a colour and permissions), bans, the **Moderation log**, the logo and a **game server** – an address members can join with a button in the header;
- **Leave community**.

> When a SlashClient admin switches groups, clans or communities off, their section says it's switched off by the administrator for now.
