# Effects

Effects are particles and pictures around your character. They are made of the game's own particles (hearts, notes, flames, runes…) or of SlashClient's own pictures (e.g. falling leaves, rings).

## Trail

Particles behind you as you walk: hearts, sparks, snow, notes, flames, clouds, fireworks, bubbles, emeralds, ash and more.

![The Trail slot](kosmetyki-trail.webp)

## Aura

Particles around you while you stand: runes, portal, cherry, souls, glow, fireflies, golden and autumn leaves and more. The **Royal** aura (Królewska) belongs to the Admin rank.

![The Aura slot](kosmetyki-aura.webp)

## Ring

A glowing ring on the ground under your feet that slowly turns. Some rings are animated. The leaf carpets lie still, and a few leaves fly up as you walk.

![The Ring slot](kosmetyki-ring.webp)

![All rings](kosmetyki-kregi-ikony.webp)

## Movement effect

Particles when you walk, jump, land, sprint, swim or shoot: footprints, a puff on jumping, a landing wave, a projectile streak, sprint sparks, a burst of bubbles, petals.

![The Movement effect slot](kosmetyki-move.webp)

## Kill effect

A short burst of particles where a mob or player you defeated falls: lightning, a burst of hearts, a firework, rising souls, confetti, gold coins, an ice shatter, a ring of fire and more. In the in-game cosmetics window you see the preview in the world.

![The Kill effect slot](kosmetyki-kill.webp)

## Who sees them

Other SlashClient players see effects. Anyone can switch them off for themselves in the HUD menu (Right Shift) → **Extras**:

| Switch | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Kill effects** → **Other players' effects** | Your and others' kill effects |
| **Rings under the feet** → **Other players' rings** | Your and others' rings |
| **Movement effects** → **Other players' effects** | Your and others' movement effects |
| **Other players' cosmetics** | All of other players' cosmetics at once |

More: [Who sees cosmetics](/wiki/kosmetyki-widocznosc).
