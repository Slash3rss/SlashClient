# Benchmark result

The launcher's benchmark measures how fast Minecraft runs on your computer. You can share the result with a link, **slashclient.pl/benchmark/…** – to compare with friends, say, or to ask for help with performance.

![A shared benchmark result (Polish interface)](strona-benchmark.webp)

## How to share

1. In the launcher open a profile and its **Performance** tab, then **Run benchmark**.
2. After the measurement click the **Share the result with your computer's specs** icon.
3. The link is copied to your clipboard.

You do not need an account, and the result is anonymous. Only hardware names are kept: processor, graphics card, amount of RAM, system and Java.

## What the page shows

| Part | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Header | graphics card and processor, the kind of measurement (**test scene** or **own world**), the date |
| Result | **average FPS**, **1% low**, **0.1% low**, the number of stutters, processor and graphics card use |
| **Scenes** | FPS in each test scene: Forest, Panorama, Village, Water, Creatures, Particles, Night |
| **Computer** | processor, graphics card, RAM (and how much the game got), system, Java |
| **Game** | Minecraft version, render distance, resolution, VSync, number of mods |

Buttons: **Copy link** and **Measure your computer** (leads to the SlashClient download).

> **1% low** is the average FPS of the slowest 1% of frames. The closer it is to the average, the smoother the game runs.

The link works for a year.
