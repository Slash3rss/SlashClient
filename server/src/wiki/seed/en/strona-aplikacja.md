# Phone app

Your friends, chats, clan and communities from the launcher are on your phone too. The app installs straight from the phone's browser – no app store. You cannot play on the phone, but you can chat with friends and get invites.

![The app on a phone (Polish interface)](strona-aplikacja-telefon.webp)

## Installing

Open [slashclient.pl/app](https://slashclient.pl/app) on your phone. On a computer, that page shows a QR code to scan with the phone's camera.

![The app page on a computer, with the QR code (Polish interface)](strona-aplikacja.webp)

- **Android**: open the address in Chrome, then the **⋮** menu → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
- **iPhone**: open the address in Safari, then **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.

Then open SlashClient from the home screen and sign in with a method linked to your account (as on the [account page](/wiki/start-konto)).

## What the app holds

| Tab | What is there |
|---|---|
| **Friends** | your friends with their statuses, adding friends by name, chats |
| **Chats** | chats, [groups](/wiki/spolecznosc-czat) and your [clan](/wiki/spolecznosc-klany), encrypted as in the launcher |
| **Communities** | your [communities](/wiki/spolecznosc-spolecznosci) and their channels |
| **Invites** | invites to be friends, to a clan and to communities, plus invites you sent |
| **Me** | status, achievements, language, notifications |

Friends see you as "Online on the phone". A game invite you get on the phone is joined later from the launcher on your computer.

## Notifications

Turn notifications on in the **Me** tab. The content of encrypted messages never goes into a notification. On an iPhone, notifications work from iOS 16.4 and only in the app added to the Home Screen.

## Chat history

The app has its own encryption key, like every launcher. To see older messages on the phone, confirm the new device in the launcher or read your history with your chat password – see [Encryption](/wiki/spolecznosc-czat).
