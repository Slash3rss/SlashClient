# Welcome to SlashClient

SlashClient is a free Minecraft launcher for Windows, macOS and Linux. It installs the game and mods, and adds its own HUD mod, friends with chat, cosmetics other SlashClient players can see, and a website with your player account.

![The SlashClient home page](strona-glowna.webp)

## What you get

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Launcher** | Installs Minecraft (official versions, Fabric and other mod loaders), keeps separate profiles with their own mods, worlds and settings, plus logs, screenshots, clips and a benchmark. |
| **SlashClient HUD** | A Fabric mod with on-screen modules (FPS, CPS, armour, keystrokes, a map and many more), its own menu and in-game extras. |
| **SlashClient account** | Friends, chat, groups, clans and communities – in the launcher, in the game and in the phone app. |
| **Cosmetics** | Capes, wings, pets, outfits, effects and more. Other SlashClient players see them on every server. |
| **Achievements and Sparks** | Playing, making friends and finishing challenges earns achievements and Sparks, which buy cosmetics in the shop. |
| **Website** | Your account, a public profile, sharing logs, screenshots and benchmark results, bug reports. |

## How much does it cost

Nothing. The launcher, the HUD, friends and the basic cosmetics are free. Some cosmetics are earned with achievements or bought with Sparks, which you also earn by playing. Sparks cannot be bought with money. You can support the project if you want to – see [Supporting the project](/wiki/strona-wsparcie).

## Where to start

1. [Download and install the launcher](/wiki/start-instalacja).
2. Go through the [first-launch wizard](/wiki/start-pierwsze-uruchomienie): language, Minecraft account, first profile.
3. [Sign in to your SlashClient account](/wiki/start-konto) to get friends and cosmetics.
4. Check [which game versions the HUD supports](/wiki/start-wersje) and the [system requirements](/wiki/start-wymagania).

Looking for something specific? See [how to use the wiki](/wiki/start-o-wiki): search, contents and the wiki in the launcher.

> Something not working? See the [FAQ](/wiki/faq-czeste-pytania).

## Will I get banned for using SlashClient?

The HUD only shows what the game already knows and gives no cheat-like advantage. Servers have their own rules, though: if a server forbids freelook, for example, turn it off in the HUD menu.
