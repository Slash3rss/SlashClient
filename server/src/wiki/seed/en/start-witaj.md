# Welcome to the SlashClient wiki

SlashClient is a free Minecraft launcher with its own HUD, friends and cosmetics. This wiki explains every feature: where to find it, how to turn it on and what you can set.

![The SlashClient wiki](start-wiki-baner.webp)

## How to read this wiki

- On the left is a list of every page, grouped by section.
- On the right are the contents of the page you are reading.
- Above the list is a search box: try *map* or *cape*.

> The wiki is in the launcher too: the **Wiki** tab → the **SlashClient** switch.

## Where to start

1. Download the launcher from the [home page](https://slashclient.pl/en#pobierz).
2. Sign in with your Microsoft account.
3. Read [how to use the wiki](/wiki/start-o-wiki).
