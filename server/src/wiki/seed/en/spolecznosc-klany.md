# Clans

A clan is a lasting team of players with its own tag next to their names, a logo and an encrypted chat. You can be in one clan at a time.

## Founding a clan

In the launcher open **Friends**, in the **Clan** section click **+** (**Found a clan**) and enter:

- a **tag** – 2–5 letters or digits, e.g. `SLX`; it has to be free (case does not matter) and must not be offensive;
- a **name** – 3–32 characters.

## Tag and logo next to names

The clan tag shows as **[TAG]** next to your name: in the launcher, and in the game on the player list (Tab) and above heads – for players with SlashClient. The owner can add a clan **logo** (PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF; the launcher shrinks it to 64×64) – then the logo shows instead of the tag.

Do not want the tag next to your name? Turn on **Hide the clan tag next to my nick**. Switch off other players' tags in the game: HUD menu (Right Shift) → **Extras** → **Logo next to names** → **Other players' clan tags**.

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | everything: invite, kick, make officers, set the logo, hand over and delete the clan |
| **Officer** | invite players and kick plain members |
| **Member** | write in the clan chat and leave |

The owner cannot leave until they hand the clan over to someone else (**Hand over clan**).

## Inviting

The owner and officers invite players by name: **Manage clan** (the gear icon) → **Invite to clan**. You can only join a clan by invite. A clan has up to 50 members (by default) and up to 20 pending invites.

## Clan chat

The clan has its own chat, end-to-end encrypted like chats with friends. In the game open it with the **Friends and chat** key (**O** by default) → **Clan**. Inviting and managing the clan happen in the launcher. See [Chat](/wiki/spolecznosc-czat).

## Achievements

There are clan achievements: **Clan member** (join a clan), **Chieftain** (found one) and **Clan power** (a clan with at least 10 members). See [Achievements](/wiki/spolecznosc-osiagniecia).

The clan also shows on your [public profile](/wiki/strona-profil).
