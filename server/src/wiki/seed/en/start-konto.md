# SlashClient account and signing in

A SlashClient account is not the same as a Minecraft account. It gives you friends, chat, groups, clans and communities, cosmetics other players can see, achievements and Sparks, and an account on the website. You can play without one – but then you see no friends and no cosmetics.

## How the account is made

The account is created by itself the first time you sign in with a Minecraft account. You never pick a password: the launcher proves to Mojang that you play with that account (the same way Minecraft servers check it), so your Minecraft token never reaches the SlashClient server.

## Signing in in the launcher

1. Add the Microsoft account that owns Minecraft in the **Accounts** tab (or in the [wizard](/wiki/start-pierwsze-uruchomienie)).
2. Open the **Friends** tab and click **Sign in with Minecraft**.

If you already have a SlashClient account and linked another sign-in method to it, the buttons below also offer **Sign in with Discord**, **Google**, **GitHub** or **Microsoft** (only the ones the server has turned on).

### Account settings in the launcher

In the **Friends** view click the gear icon, **Account and profile**. It holds:

- **Sign-in:** – the sign-in methods linked to the account. Click a grey badge to link a new one (e.g. Discord, to sign in on the website), or a green one to unlink it.
- **Accounts** – the Minecraft accounts linked to your SlashClient account. Each can have [its own set of cosmetics](/wiki/kosmetyki-zestawy).
- **Privacy and notifications**, including **Show friends which server I play on**.
- **Streams** – [Twitch and Kick](/wiki/spolecznosc-streamy).
- **Profile on the website** – your [public profile](/wiki/strona-profil) and its privacy.
- **Chat encryption** – devices and the key backup, see [Chat](/wiki/spolecznosc-czat).

## Signing in on the website

On [slashclient.pl/account](https://slashclient.pl/account) click **Sign in** and pick the method you linked in the launcher (e.g. **Sign in with Discord**). You cannot create an account on the website – it is always made in the launcher.

![Signing in on the account page](strona-konto-logowanie.webp)

What the account page holds is described in [Account on the website](/wiki/strona-konto).

## Signing in on a phone

The [phone app](/wiki/strona-aplikacja) signs in the same way as the website: with a method linked to your account.

## Signing in in the game

There is no separate sign-in in the game. When the launcher starts the game and you are signed in on the **Friends** tab, the SlashClient HUD mod uses that account: friends, chat and cosmetics just work.

> Does the game say "Sign in in the launcher (Friends tab) to wear SlashClient cosmetics"? Sign in in the launcher and start the game again.
