# Communities

A community is a big group of players (up to 1000) with text channels, roles and moderation – a bit like a Discord server, but inside SlashClient. You can be in 10 communities and found 3 (by default).

> Community chat is **not** end-to-end encrypted: it is stored on the server and moderators can read it. Keep private matters to chats with friends.

![A community channel in the phone app (Polish interface)](spolecznosc-spolecznosci-czat.webp)

## Where it is

In the launcher: **Friends** → the **Communities** section, with these buttons:

- **Join with an invite** – paste an invite link or code;
- **Discover communities** – search public communities;
- **Create a community**.

In the game, communities are under the **Friends and chat** key (channels, reactions, polls), and on a phone in the **Communities** tab of the [app](/wiki/strona-aplikacja). Managing (members, requests, bans) happens in the launcher.

![The list of communities in the phone app (Polish interface)](spolecznosc-spolecznosci-lista.webp)

## Creating one

Give it a name (3–40 characters), optionally a description (up to 500 characters) and a tag (2–6 characters, shown as #TAG – never next to names). Then pick:

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| Visibility | **Public** – listed in Discover, with a [page on slashclient.pl](/wiki/strona-spolecznosc); **Private** – invite only, hidden |
| Joining | **Open** – anyone joins right away; **On request** – moderators accept |

## Channels

Every community has the **General** and **Announcements** channels (only admins post there). You can add your own text channels – up to 20 in all – and choose which roles read and write in them.

## Roles

The built-in roles are owner, admin, moderator and member. On top of that, up to 15 roles of your own (e.g. VIP) with a colour and permissions: pinning, deleting messages, muting, kicking, banning, invites, join requests, reports, the moderation log, slow mode, managing channels and roles.

## In the chat

- replies and emoji reactions;
- pinned messages (up to 50 per channel);
- **@name** mentions;
- polls (2–10 answers);
- pictures and [GIFs](/wiki/spolecznosc-czat);
- **Report message** – it goes to the moderators;
- notifications: all messages, **mentions only** or none.

Moderators have slow mode (from 5 seconds to an hour), timeouts of up to 7 days, bans and a **Moderation log**.

## Invites

Community admins make invite links: valid for 30 minutes, an hour, a day, 7 days or forever, for 1, 5, 10, 25, 100 or unlimited uses. A link works for a private community too. Opened in a browser it shows an invite page – see [Community page and invites](/wiki/strona-spolecznosc).

## Other

- **Game server** – a community can show its Minecraft server's address and how many people play there now.
- **Verified community** – a badge given by the SlashClient team.
- **Show on my profile** – whether the community shows on your [public profile](/wiki/strona-profil).
