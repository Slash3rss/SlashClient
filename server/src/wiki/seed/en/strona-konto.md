# Account on the website

On [slashclient.pl/account](https://slashclient.pl/account) you change cosmetics, set your profile's privacy and see your achievements – without starting the launcher. Sign in with a method linked to your account (e.g. Discord) – see [Signing in](/wiki/start-konto).

The page has tabs: **Profile**, **Cosmetics**, **Privacy**, **Streams**, **Support** and – for partners – **Partner server**. Each has its own address (e.g. `/account#prywatnosc`), so you can link straight to it.

## Profile

![The Profile tab (Polish interface)](strona-konto-profil.webp)

- Avatar, name, `@handle`, sign-in methods, ranks and your [Sparks](/wiki/spolecznosc-iskry).
- **Avatar:** – the face from one of your Minecraft accounts' skins, or the picture from Discord, Google and so on.
- **Badge next to the nick:** – which [rank](/wiki/spolecznosc-rangi) shows next to your name, or **No rank badge**. With several Minecraft accounts you can set it for each one (**In game as …**).
- **Minecraft:** – linked Minecraft accounts; a click unlinks one (the page asks first).
- **Show friends which server I play on**.
- **Sign out**.
- The **Achievements and Sparks** card – every [achievement](/wiki/spolecznosc-osiagniecia) with your progress, and the latest balance changes.

## Cosmetics

Every slot and [set](/wiki/kosmetyki-zestawy) with a 3D preview. Hovering tries an item on, a click puts it on, and a priced tile lets you buy the item for Sparks. In the **Set for:** list you choose whether you change the whole account's set or one Minecraft account's. See [Cosmetics: how to wear them](/wiki/kosmetyki-wstep).

![The Cosmetics tab (Polish interface)](strona-konto-kosmetyki.webp)

## Privacy

Who sees your [public profile](/wiki/strona-profil) and what is on it, plus **Show me in friend suggestions** (see [Friends](/wiki/spolecznosc-znajomi)).

![The Privacy tab (Polish interface)](strona-konto-prywatnosc.webp)

## Streams

Linking your Twitch and Kick channels, and notification settings – see [Streams](/wiki/spolecznosc-streamy).

## Support

Your support tier and how long it lasts, the **Support the project** and **Manage subscription** buttons (for monthly support), the **Show the supporter badge on my profile** and **Show me on the supporters wall** switches, and **Payment history** with receipts and refund requests. See [Supporting the project](/wiki/strona-wsparcie).

![The Support tab (Polish interface)](strona-konto-wsparcie.webp)

## Partner server

A tab only for partner accounts that have a partner server. It sets how that server looks in the in-game server list for SlashClient players:

- **Background** – a wide picture behind the server on the list (PNG or JPG up to 3 MB, about 1200×120 works best): **Upload a background**, **Change the background**, **Remove the background**;
- **Animated icon** – a GIF or APNG instead of the server's normal icon.
