# Achievements and challenges

Playing with SlashClient earns achievements and gives you challenges. Each one pays [Sparks](/wiki/spolecznosc-iskry) once, and some also give a cape you cannot get any other way.

## Where it is

- **Launcher**: the **Achievements** tab – with the **Achievements**, **Challenges** and **Recommend** tabs.
- **Game**: a notification in the corner of the screen when you unlock an achievement or finish a challenge.
- **Website**: on your [account](/wiki/strona-konto), in the **Profile** tab – the **Achievements and Sparks** card; your achievements also show on your [public profile](/wiki/strona-profil).
- Open a friend's achievements with the **Achievements** button in your chat with them.

## Achievement categories

Achievement names are shown in English in the English interface; a few examples:

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Play** | First hour, Warming up (10 h, Storm cape), Veteran (100 h, a cape), A month of play (30 days, Inferno cape), A long day, Weekend player, Anniversary, challenges (Challenge hunter, Checker cape) |
| **Streaks** | Three in a row, A week in a row … A whole year in a row – with streak capes |
| **Friends** | First friend, High five (Ice cape), First message, Chatterbox, Over here! (send a map waypoint), Assemble! (start a party) |
| **Invites** | for [recommending SlashClient](/wiki/spolecznosc-polecenia), with capes |
| **Groups and clans** | Better together, Clan member, Chieftain, Clan power, Part of the community |
| **Servers** | Traveller (5 servers), Tourist (10, Lava cape) … Server cartographer (100) |
| **Partner servers** | for time played on a partner server, each with a cape of its own |
| **Sharing** | Photographer (share a screenshot), Sharing is caring, Head in the clouds, Street artist (a spray) |
| **Style** | Dapper (wear 5 cosmetics), First purchase, Collector, A spark of hope (Honey cape), A small collection |
| **Hidden** | shown as "???" until you get them – e.g. for playing at night or on holidays |

A bar under each achievement shows your progress.

### How play time counts

- It counts the game started from the SlashClient launcher, in a world or on a server (not in the menu).
- A day of play is a day with at least 10 minutes of play. Days follow UTC.
- At most 12 hours count per day.

## Challenges

Every day you get 3 daily challenges, and every week 3 weekly ones. Daily ones renew at midnight Polish time, weekly ones on Monday.

| Example | Reward |
|---|---|
| Play 30 min | 15 Sparks |
| Play on 2 different servers | 15 Sparks |
| Send 5 messages to friends | 10 Sparks |
| Vote for a server in the launcher | 15 Sparks |
| Play 300 min this week | 100 Sparks |
| Play at least 10 min on 4 different days | 80 Sparks |

Finishing all of a day's challenges gives a bonus (30 Sparks by default), and all of a week's – 100 Sparks. The **Challenges** tab shows **Today**, **This week** and the time until new challenges.

> The pool of challenges and the rewards can change – the SlashClient team sets them.
