# Sparks and the shop

**Sparks** (✦, Iskry in Polish) are SlashClient's currency. You earn them by playing and spend them on cosmetics in the shop. Sparks cannot be bought with money, given to other players or traded.

## Where Sparks come from

| Source | How many |
|---|---|
| [Achievements](/wiki/spolecznosc-osiagniecia) | from 5 to 1000 per achievement, once |
| Daily and weekly [challenges](/wiki/spolecznosc-osiagniecia) | 10–100 per challenge, plus a bonus for finishing them all |
| [Recommending SlashClient](/wiki/spolecznosc-polecenia) | 100 for each counted referral; your friend gets 50 |

## Where you see your balance

- **Launcher** – in the top right corner of the window, on every tab. A click opens the **Shop**.
- **Game** – in the corner of the **Cosmetics** window.
- **Website** – on your [account](/wiki/strona-konto): next to your name and in the **Achievements and Sparks** card.

## The shop

In the launcher click your Sparks (the **Shop** button). The **Shop** view has these tabs:

- **Cosmetics** – items with a price: capes, wings, elytras, rings, sprays, outfits, shields, weapons, auras and trails;
- **Sets** – [sets](/wiki/kosmetyki-zestawy) whose missing parts you can buy;
- **Sparks history** – where your Sparks came from and what they went on, with the balance after each change.

You can also buy straight from the cosmetics: in the launcher (**Accounts**), in the game (**Esc → Cosmetics**) and on the website – click a priced tile. Before buying you see how many you have and how many will be left.

> A bought item stays on your account for good. The SlashClient team sets the prices and they can change.
