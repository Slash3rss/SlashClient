# Friends and chat

The **Friends** tab connects you with other SlashClient players: see who's playing and where, chat, invite friends to play and join them in one click.

![Friends and chat](launcher-znajomi.webp)

## Your SlashClient account

Friends, chat, achievements, the shop and the cloud run on a **SlashClient account**. It's a separate account and doesn't change your Minecraft accounts.

**How to sign in:** Friends → **Sign in with Minecraft** (when the launcher has a Microsoft account) or **Sign in with Discord / Microsoft / Google / GitHub**. The account is created on your first sign-in. You can link more sign-in methods and Minecraft accounts later under **Account and profile**.

## Your strip

At the top of the list is your name with your rank and clan tag, and next to it:

- the status dot – **Online**, **Away**, **Do not disturb** or **Invisible** (Do not disturb hides every notification except achievements);
- the gear, **Account and profile** – account and privacy settings;
- sign out.

## The list

The list has sections:

- **Invites** – friend requests (accept / decline);
- **Suggestions** – SlashClient players you recently played with on the same server or share friends with (add, or don't suggest again);
- **Clan**, **Groups** and **Communities** – see [Groups, clans and communities](/wiki/launcher-spolecznosci);
- **Friends** – in-game first, then online; under each name you see what they're doing ("Minecraft 1.21.4 · Fabric · on play.example.pl", "in the menu", "on the phone", "Offline · …");
- **Sent invites** – your requests that are still waiting (cancel the invite).

To add a friend, type their name into the **Friend's name** box and click invite.

## Chatting with a friend

Click a friend to open the chat. Messages to friends are **end-to-end encrypted** – the SlashClient server can't read them.

- **Write a message…** (up to 2000 characters), pictures (also pasted with Ctrl+V) and **GIFs and stickers** when the server offers them.
- For a short while you can **Edit** or **Delete** your own message (the "…" menu next to it).
- A **Read** mark appears under messages.
- Waypoints shared in the game show as a card with a **Copy coordinates** button.

Buttons in the chat header:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Join** | joins the server your friend is playing on |
| Join the world | enters the world your friend opened to LAN (through the SlashClient server, no port forwarding) |
| **Watch** | opens the stream when your friend is live |
| **Invite to play** | sends an invite to a server of your choice |
| **Show profile** | your friend's profile on the website |
| **Media** | every picture and link from the conversation, with picture storage and limits |
| **Achievements** | your friend's achievements |
| **Mute notifications** | no notifications from this person |
| **Remove friend** | removes the friend |

## Party

The **Invite a party** button (next to adding friends) invites several friends at once (up to 7) to one server. The party card shows who has joined; invited friends get join, decline and leave buttons, and the leader can disband the party.

## Account and profile

The **Account and profile** window (the gear next to your name) has several parts:

- **Privacy and notifications**: show friends which server you're on, notify when friends start playing, **Show my position on friends' minimap** (nobody, all friends or chosen friends – only on the same server and in the same dimension, and the position is never stored) and **Show friends what I use** (mods, packs, shaders and servers for the "Popular among friends" strips);
- **Chat encryption**: your devices, handing chat history to a new device, and a **chat password** – a key backup that lets you read your history on a new computer;
- **Accounts**: linked sign-in methods and Minecraft accounts;
- **Streams**: link a Twitch or Kick channel, get notified when friends go live, hide your own streams;
- **Profile on the website**: a link to your profile and **Profile privacy** (who can see it and what's on it);
- **Badge next to your nick**: which rank to show next to your name.

## Recommend SlashClient

The **Recommend SlashClient** button at the top of the tab opens your referral code and link. A friend enters the code within a few days of creating their account; once they link a Minecraft account and play the required time, you both get Sparks, and more referrals also earn you capes. The window also lists **Invited friends** and has a **Got a code from a friend?** box. Details in [Achievements, challenges and Sparks](/wiki/launcher-osiagniecia).

![The Recommend SlashClient window](launcher-polec.webp)

## Notifications

While the launcher is in the background, new messages, invites and friend requests show up as system notifications. Clicking one brings the launcher up on the right chat. The **Friends** tab shows an unread counter. Turn notifications off in [Settings](/wiki/launcher-ustawienia).
