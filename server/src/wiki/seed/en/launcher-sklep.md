# Shop

In the **Shop** you buy cosmetics with Sparks (✦), which you earn in [Achievements](/wiki/launcher-osiagniecia). Bought cosmetics stay on your account for good. The shop doesn't take real money.

**Where:** your Sparks balance with a **Shop** button in the top-right corner of the launcher, on every tab, while you're signed in to SlashClient. The **Shop** link in the cosmetics menu ([Accounts](/wiki/launcher-konta) tab) leads there too.

The shop has three tabs: **Cosmetics**, **Sets** and **Sparks history**.

## Cosmetics

![The shop's cosmetics](launcher-sklep.webp)

Each tile shows a picture, the name, the kind (Cape, Spray, Ears & horns, Trail…) and the price.

1. Click the price on a tile.
2. The **Buy …?** window shows the item, the price and your balance after buying.
3. **Buy for ✦ …** completes the purchase. Sparks can't be refunded.

Bought items show **Owned** and a **Put on** button; when you're short of Sparks, the tile says how many you still need.

## Sets

A set is a group of items from several slots (cape, wings, ears, tail, shield…) that go together, such as Dragon, Demon, Angel, Pirate, Knight, Autumn, Wolf or Cat.

![Sets in the shop](launcher-sklep-zestawy.webp)

- The **4/7** counter shows how many parts you have.
- Missing parts are dimmed, with a lock and the price; a list below says how to get them (in the shop for Sparks, from an achievement, for a rank or for project supporters).
- Clicking a missing part that's for sale buys it right away (after you confirm).
- **Wear the set** puts on every part you own at once. When it's all on already, the button says it's worn.

## Sparks history

Every change of your balance: achievements, challenges, referrals, admin rewards and purchases, with the date, the amount and the balance after the change.

![Sparks history](launcher-sklep-historia.webp)

> When an admin switches the shop off, the balance in the corner disappears and **Sparks history** moves back to a tab in Achievements.
