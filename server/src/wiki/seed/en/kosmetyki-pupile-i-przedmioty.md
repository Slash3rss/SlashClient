# Pets, items and sprays

## Pet

A small companion flies at your shoulder. Most pets are shrunken mobs from the game (allay, vex, bat, parrot, bee, blaze, phantom, axolotl, frog, fox, cat), and some are SlashClient's own: **Haze the ghost** (Duszek Haze) and wheelbarrows.

![The Pet slot](kosmetyki-pet.webp)

## Shield

How the shield in your hand looks. Your inventory and the shield's behaviour stay the same – it is only the look.

![The Shield slot](kosmetyki-shield.webp)

## Weapon skin

How every sword (sword skins, e.g. a light saber, a katana, a flaming sword) or the bow (bow skins, e.g. the dragon bow) looks. Inventory, name and behaviour stay the same.

![The Weapon skin slot](kosmetyki-weapon.webp)

![Shields and weapon skins](kosmetyki-bronie-ikony.webp)

> For fair PvP, other players see your shield and weapon only when they turn on **Other players' skins** themselves in the HUD menu (Right Shift) → **Extras** → **Shield and weapon skins**. It is off by default. You always see your own skins.

## Spray

A picture you paint with a key on the block in front of you (up to 5 blocks away). It disappears after a minute. SlashClient players on the same server see it.

How to use it:

1. Put a spray on in the **Spray** slot.
2. Set the **Spray** key – sprays have no key by default. Do it in the in-game cosmetics window (the **Key** field), in SlashClient's keys, or in the game's Controls.
3. In the game, look at a block and press the key.

![The Spray slot](kosmetyki-spray.webp)

![All sprays](kosmetyki-spraye-ikony.webp)

Switch off other players' sprays in the HUD menu → **Extras** → **Sprays** → **Other players' sprays**.

## Prop

Something you push in front of you with both hands while they are free – for now wheelbarrows (plain, golden and with flowers). With an item in your hand, while eating, swimming or flying, the prop hides.

![The Prop slot](kosmetyki-prop.webp)

## How to put them on

Launcher: **Accounts** → **Cosmetics** → **SlashClient** and the slot; in the game **Esc → Cosmetics**; on the website [Account](https://slashclient.pl/account) → **Cosmetics**. See also [How to get cosmetics](/wiki/kosmetyki-zdobywanie).
