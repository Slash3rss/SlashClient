# How to get cosmetics

Every cosmetic is got in one of a few ways. One you do not have yet shows a lock, and hovering over it tells you how to get it.

![A player's capes: free, locked and priced in Sparks (Polish interface)](kosmetyki-zdobywanie.webp)

## Free

Most cosmetics can be worn right away, without a lock: among them the first eight capes and many wings, elytras, trails, auras, pets, outfits, suits and effects.

## For achievements

Some items (mostly capes) come only with a particular [achievement](/wiki/spolecznosc-osiagniecia), e.g. 10 hours of play, 5 friends, a streak of days, referred friends or finished challenges. They cannot be bought. When you unlock the achievement, the item is on your account at once, and in the game a notification says "new cape".

![An achievement notification with a new cape (Polish interface)](spolecznosc-osiagniecie-powiadomienie.webp)

## From the shop for Sparks

Items with a price (✦) are bought with [Sparks](/wiki/spolecznosc-iskry) – the currency you earn with achievements, challenges and recommending SlashClient to friends. Sparks cannot be bought with money. A bought item stays on your account for good.

Where to buy:

- **Launcher**: click your Sparks in the top right corner of the window (**Shop**) – a view of its own with the **Cosmetics**, **Sets** and **Sparks history** tabs. You can also click a priced tile in **Accounts** → **Cosmetics**.
- **Game**: in the **Cosmetics** window click a priced tile. The game asks whether to buy it and shows how many Sparks will be left.
- **Website**: on your [account](https://slashclient.pl/account), in the **Cosmetics** tab, click a priced tile.

![Buying a cape in the game (Polish interface)](kosmetyki-gra-kup.webp)

The admins set the prices and they can change; the tile always shows the current one.

## For ranks

Some items belong to a rank: **Admin**, **Support**, **Partner** or **Friend** (e.g. the capes in the ranks' colours, the Phoenix and Crystal wings, the Royal aura). The tile shows a shield in the rank's colour. The SlashClient team gives out ranks.

## For supporters

[Supporters of the project](/wiki/strona-wsparcie) get their tier's items while their support lasts:

| Tier | Item |
|---|---|
| Supporter | the **SlashClient Heart** cape (Serce SlashClient) |
| Supporter+ | plus the **Patron's wings** elytra (Skrzydła mecenasa) |
| Patron | plus the golden **Patron** cape (Mecenas) |

These items cannot be bought with Sparks.

## Exclusive

The admins can make any item exclusive and give it to chosen players (e.g. for a contest). Such an item shows a lock saying "exclusive, given by the admins".
