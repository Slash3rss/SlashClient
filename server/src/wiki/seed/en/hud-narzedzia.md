# Tools: XP calculator and Minecraft Wiki

## XP calculator

An experience calculator in the game. **Right Shift → Settings → Extras → XP calculator**.

![The XP calculator](hud-kalkulator-xp.webp)

- **Level → XP points** – how many points a level is and how many more to the next.
- **XP points → level** – the level some points make and how far into it.
- **XP cost between levels** – the points needed from one level to another.
- **My level** – fills in your current level and points.

Everything is worked out as you type.

## Minecraft Wiki in game

The [Minecraft Wiki](https://minecraft.wiki) search and articles in a game window, in English or Polish (Minecraft Wiki Polska) – a button in the window switches the language. **Recently read** remembers what you read. **In the browser** opens the article outside the game.

**How to open it:** the **Minecraft Wiki** card in Extras or the "Minecraft Wiki" key (none by default). The **"Minecraft Wiki: about this item"** key opens the article about what you hold, look at, or point at with the mouse in an inventory.

The wiki is loaded by the SlashClient launcher, so it works in a game started from the launcher.
