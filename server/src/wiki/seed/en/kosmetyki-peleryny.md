# Capes, wings and elytras

## Capes

A SlashClient cape hangs on your back like a normal Minecraft cape and replaces it. Other SlashClient players see it on every server. There are more than 60 of them, and new ones keep coming.

![The Cape slot on the account page](kosmetyki-cape.webp)

### Free

Everyone has the first eight capes right away: SlashClient, Ocean, Ember, Forest, Galaxy, Rose, Gold and Mono.

![Free capes](kosmetyki-peleryny-darmowe.webp)

### For achievements

Many capes come only with [achievements](/wiki/spolecznosc-osiagniecia) – for example **Storm** (Burza) for 10 hours of play, **Ice** (Lód) for five friends, **Sakura** for your first referred friend, **Night** (Noc) for playing at night, and the flame capes with a number for playing several days in a row (7, 14, 30, 60, 100 and 365 days). Achievements for playing on partner servers have capes of their own too.

![Capes for achievements](kosmetyki-peleryny-osiagniecia.webp)

> Capes that were free for everyone until version 0.9.69 stay for good on every account that existed before that change.

### From the shop

Some capes are sold in the [shop](/wiki/spolecznosc-iskry) for Sparks you earn in the game. The admins set the prices and they can change – the tile shows the current one.

![Capes in the shop (prices in Sparks)](kosmetyki-peleryny-sklep.webp)

### For ranks and supporters

Each rank has a cape in its colour (Admin, Support, Partner, Friend), and [supporters of the project](/wiki/strona-wsparcie) get the **SlashClient Heart** cape (Serce SlashClient), and from the Patron tier the golden **Patron** cape (Mecenas).

![Rank and supporter capes](kosmetyki-peleryny-rangi.webp)

### Animated

Some capes are animated: they wave, flicker or shine. They have from a few to more than a dozen frames that the game plays in a loop.

![Frames of animated capes](kosmetyki-peleryny-animowane.webp)

## Wings

Wings are worn on your back in the cape's place: folded while you stand and spread in flight, like an elytra. When you put on a real elytra, the elytra shows. There are angel, demon, dragon, butterfly, frost and bone wings and more; some are in the shop, and the **Phoenix** and **Crystal** wings belong to the Partner and Support ranks.

![Wings on the character](kosmetyki-wings.webp)

## Elytras

The look of a real elytra when you put one on. Without this cosmetic the elytra shows your cape's pattern. There are Feathers, Neon, Void, Nebula, Magma, Autumn and more, the shop has Golden Glow, Glacier and others, and supporters from the Supporter+ tier get the **Patron's wings**.

![An elytra on the character](kosmetyki-elytra.webp)

## How to put them on

- **Launcher**: Accounts → Cosmetics → SlashClient → **Cape**, **Wings** or **Elytra**.
- **Game**: Esc → **Cosmetics**.
- **Website**: [Account](https://slashclient.pl/account) → **Cosmetics**.

More: [Cosmetics: how to wear them](/wiki/kosmetyki-wstep).

> Wearing something in the **Back** slot (a backpack, a guitar…)? In the game it shows instead of the cape and the wings.
