## 1. Who runs the support {#who}

1. Support is accepted by {{admin.name}}, {{admin.address}}, {{admin.nip}}, contact: {{admin.email}} and via the report form at [/bugs](/bugs) (the "Organiser"). SlashClient is not affiliated with Mojang Studios or Microsoft.
2. The Organiser runs support as an unregistered activity (Article 5(1) of the Polish Entrepreneurs' Law of 6 March 2018). Towards consumers the Organiser has the same duties as a business, including those under the Polish Consumer Rights Act of 30 May 2014.
3. Matters not covered here are governed by the [Terms of Service](/terms) and the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## 2. What support is {#what}

1. Support is voluntary. The launcher, the mod and the SlashClient services remain free; nothing is taken away from free players.
2. As a thank-you, supporters receive digital perks assigned to their SlashClient account for the duration of the support: the "Supporter" rank (or higher) next to their name, selected cosmetics, marks on the public profile and higher limits for cloud profiles and shared screenshots. The current tiers are described at [/support](/support).
3. Support gives no in-game advantages, includes no random rewards or "loot boxes" and cannot be used to buy Sparks (the achievements currency).
4. The contract is concluded when the operator confirms the payment. The perks start right after the confirmation.

## 3. Tiers, price and duration {#tiers}

1. Prices on the website are final prices in Polish złoty, or in euro where the website shows a euro price. The Organiser is not an active VAT payer (exemption under Article 113(1) of the Polish VAT Act), so no VAT or other charges are added to the price.
2. One-time support gives perks for the stated number of days from payment confirmation; further support of the same tier extends that time. Afterwards the profile may keep a "Supported" mark unless the supporter turns it off.
3. Monthly support is a subscription renewed automatically every month at the same price until cancelled. Perks last for the paid period; if a renewal is late, they stay for a few more days (grace period).
4. An unregistered activity has a quarterly revenue limit. Once the support for a quarter is in, new payments are paused until the start of the next quarter (the website shows this). Running subscriptions and paid periods carry on.

## 4. Payments and confirmations {#payments}

1. Payments are handled by external operators: Stripe (card, BLIK, Przelewy24, Apple Pay, Google Pay) and PayPal, on their own pages and under their own terms. The Organiser neither sees nor stores card or payment account details.
2. Perks are granted once the operator confirms the payment (usually within seconds, longer for some methods).
3. Support goes to the SlashClient account the supporter was signed in to when paying.
4. The operator sends the payment confirmation; the payment history is also on the [account](/account#wsparcie) page. On request sent to {{admin.email}} the Organiser issues a bill (rachunek).

## 5. Cancelling a subscription {#cancelling}

A subscription can be cancelled at any time on the account page ("Manage subscription", Stripe's portal). Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period; perks stay until then.

## 6. Right of withdrawal and refunds {#refunds}

1. A consumer may withdraw from the contract within 14 days of concluding it (the payment confirmation) without giving a reason. The Organiser does not take this right away, even once the perks are running. For a subscription the period runs from the first payment.
2. In addition, the Organiser refunds any payment (including a further month of a subscription) requested for refund within 14 days of that payment, with no reason needed.
3. How to withdraw or ask for a refund: [account](/account#wsparcie) → Support the project → Request a refund, an e-mail to {{admin.email}} or a letter to the Organiser's address. You may use the model form in point 11, but you do not have to. Sending the statement before the period ends is enough.
4. The Organiser refunds the money within 14 days of receiving the statement, through the payment operator, by the same method used to pay, at no cost to the supporter. A refund removes the perks granted for that payment.
5. Disputing a payment with the operator (chargeback) also removes the perks granted for that payment.

## 7. Minors {#minors}

1. Many SlashClient players are minors. A person aged 13–17 may support the project only with the consent of a parent or guardian; this must be confirmed before paying.
2. For a person under 13, support may be bought only by a parent or guardian, with their own payment method.
3. The Organiser does not check age or consent other than through this confirmation. A parent or guardian who did not consent may request a refund the same way (the refund form has a separate option for this) — also after the 14 days.

## 8. Complaints and contact {#complaints}

1. If the perks do not work as described (e.g. the rank did not appear), write to {{admin.email}} or via [/bugs](/bugs), giving the account name, the payment date and a description of the problem.
2. The Organiser replies within 14 days. Consumers have the rights for lack of conformity of digital content and services with the contract under the Consumer Rights Act.
3. Out-of-court dispute resolution is described in the [Terms of Service](/terms#disputes).

## 9. Personal data {#data}

The Organiser processes SlashClient account data and payment information (amount, date, tier, operator identifiers) to grant perks, for accounting, the sales record and to handle refunds. The supporters wall shows only people who turned it on themselves. Details — legal bases, retention and your rights — are in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## 10. Changes and ending {#changes}

1. The Organiser may change tiers and prices for the future; this does not affect periods already paid for. A change of a subscription's price is announced at least 30 days in advance; you may cancel then.
2. If the project ends, active subscriptions will be cancelled and the Organiser will refund the proportional part of the price for the unused part of the paid period.
